**The typed-ASP face = `adsmt-ir-asp`** — the closed-world / least-model / stable-model SIBLING of the SMT-LIB face ([[typed-cic-ir]] §5 faces), both 1:1 subsets of the unified surface elaborating to the same kernel IR; the kernel `def`(closed-world) vs `open`(classical/theory) Modality is the hook (rules→`def` atoms; SMT theory atoms→`open`; they cooperate). **DECIDED 2026-06-25** after a 2-workflow design exploration (grounded-in-adsmt-code `asp-face-design` 8 agents + cross-language feature mining `asp-face-language-mining` AQL/SQL/Haskell/Scala3 5 agents) — the two CONVERGED on the same spine (strong cross-validation).

**THESIS: theory-first + abduction-native** (plain typed-Datalog would be "a worse Datalog"). What this substrate uniquely gives: (1) an ASP rule body can carry `open` SMT theory atoms (Int/EUF/datatype) decided by the SAME engine — the ASP⊕SMT seam; (2) **abduction is the exact DUAL of a rule**, fully merged, not bolted on.

**Surface = a NEGATION LADDER** the elaborator infers the level of and structurally REFUSES to outrun (parses-but-abstains above the rung whose gate exists): **L0** definite typed Datalog → **L1 theory interior** (`open` SMT atoms in bodies — the differentiator, shipped 2nd not last) → L2 stratified `not` + integrity constraints `:- B` → **L3 full stable models** (GL reduct) → L4 choice/disjunction → L5 aggregates/optimization → **L6 1st-class abduction**.

**The SPINE (both workflows independently):** (a) **def/open modality boundary** = `not p` legal only on stratified `def` (closed-world=not-derivable); `open` predicate `not`→theory negation; elaborator rejects wrong-modality `not`. (b) **verified-θ as a type** (Scala `given`/`using`): the GL reduct demands a G-SAT-verified θ in scope — makes the unsound "naive AND of two gates" inexpressible; undecided `open` atoms = Kleene-undefined (SQL 3VL)→Unknown. (c) **sort inference (Haskell HM) + `CanEq` typed equality** (Scala multiversal): 1st-order fragment needs no annotations; every `=` routes via `CanEq[σ,τ]` to the right kernel equality (EUF/arith/conv) — kills the recurring EUF↔arith cross-sort bug class at elaboration time.

**ABDUCTIVE MERGE mechanism (both workflows agree): abducible = a `def`-completion-LESS `Open` atom** (the exact opposite of a `def` atom whose Clark completion `p↔body` forces false-by-default). **Zero new `Modality` variant, zero gate change.** The abductive engine's dormant `HornRule`/`SchematicHornRule` base is re-homed under `adsmt-ir-asp` as THE ASP rule type → **one rule IR, two directions** (forward least-fixpoint = answer-set membership; backward SLD = abduction relevance grounder). `minimize`/`rank`/`dedup`/cycle-guard reused. **Deduction = empty-abducible abduction** ("entailed(G) ⟺ abduce(G)={}"). `:abduct-consistency` becomes intrinsic (answer sets are consistent by construction); SMT-LIB `(abduce)`/`(get-abduct)` wire stays compatible (Verus/cvc5). Confirmed dead-code seam: `Solver::abduce` builds `SldEngine::new(&abducibles)` (abducibles only, no rules); lu-kb shim stubs `Rule`/`Abduce` — wire `SldEngine::with_all(&abducibles, &rules)`.

**CLOSED-WORLD vs CONSTRUCTIVE-KERNEL resolution (the hard question):** they do NOT unify — same generate-and-check firewall as the SMT face. **Kernel certifies typing + POSITIVE derivations only; an untrusted solver + a re-checkable stability gate (lfp-of-reduct ⊳ θ) certify closed-world negation + stability; the two never share a judgement.** Membership `p(c)∈M` = a constructive proof object (literal under the `Inductive` carrier); negation is a side-condition on the external model `M`, discharged by the gate, never a kernel theorem. Clark completion is the ONLY sanctioned closed-world step into the kernel (explicit iff-def the face commits to). **MVP (definite) sidesteps it entirely**: no negation ⇒ least model = stable model = completion, all re-checkable by one fixpoint.

**lu-kb = the shared live KB substrate** both halves read from (rule/abduce/constraint/enum/data as item kinds in one typed file) = the literal realization of "merge abductive entirely"; activates two dead paths with one wire. (Runner-up: lu-kb as AOT-banked compiled-KB artifact — deferred.)

**Highest-leverage cross-language picks (beyond plain typed-ASP):** 1st-order **matching over kernel datatypes** (Haskell ADT-match + Scala extractor; biggest expressiveness jump, `rule_base.rs` hook exists, consumes idle kernel datatypes); **`summon`-with-holes** unified abductive engine (Scala given-resolution; sort-pre-filtered `2^n` search; `Derivation` inductive = re-checkable cert into adsmt-cert); **stratified aggregates + comprehension** (SQL GROUP BY/AQL COLLECT; aggregate value = LIA term = 2nd ASP⊕SMT seam); **`def rec` named scoped recursion + AQL fuel band** (SQL WITH RECURSIVE; bound-exhaustion→Unknown). **Dangerous-but-tempting (defer/gate):** choice/cardinality→multi-model (rc.38/#277 pigeonhole honesty); recursive/non-monotone aggregates (SQL forbids for a reason — borrowed restrictions ARE the sound slice); full dependent inference (undecidable→bidirectional only).

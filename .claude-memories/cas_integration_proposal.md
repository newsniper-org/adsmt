**User proposal (2026-06-28) — a v1.0.0-stable PRE-RELEASE review item (NOT scheduled yet; surface it during the final feature-review before the rc.N→1.0.0 cut, [[pre-stable-feature-freeze]]/[[feedback-stable-signoff-user-approval]]):** integrate **Computer Algebra Systems** as backends/oracles. Candidates the user named: **Singular** (Gröbner/commutative algebra — already a `~/verus-fork` dep for `integer_ring`, see [[verus-integer-ring-setup]]), **Pari/GP** (number theory), **Axiom**, **Cadabra** (tensor/field-theory algebra), **GAP** (group theory / discrete algebra), **Mathics** (open Mathematica-like), **Giac** (general CAS). 

**Why it's natural here:** adsmt already has the *delegation oracle* pattern (the `lu-smt`→OxiZ in-process/subprocess fallback, [[oxiz-relationship]]) and the *prover-emit package manager* (wasmi-run emitter packages, [[adsmt-emit-system]]) — a CAS backend would slot in as either (a) a delegation target for obligations the native/OxiZ engines can't decide (polynomial-ideal membership, factorization, algebraic-number arithmetic, Gröbner-based QE, group/tensor identities), or (b) emitter/oracle packages in the PM model. The session that prompted this just used Singular (via Verus `integer_ring`) to discharge the *Like-family complex reduction algebra ([[numberlike-family-design]]) that nlsat couldn't — exactly the class of obligation a first-class CAS integration would cover at SOLVE time (not just 선검증 time).

**Tie-ins worth weighing at review:** the `*Like` family / four-way interlock ([[four-way-interlock-design-intent]]) wants ring/field algebra at solve time (G2 currently abstains until oxiz-nl2 on the bus — a CAS could be the bus member); oxiz-nl2's CAD/resultant work ([[oxiz-nlsat-redesign]]) overlaps Singular/Giac territory; abduction could use CAS for algebraic hypothesis synthesis. **Open questions for the review:** soundness boundary (a CAS is an UNTRUSTED oracle → its results must be re-checked or downgrade like the div/mod Sat→Unknown gate, cf. #291); which CAS for which obligation class; build/dependency footprint (subprocess vs FFI vs wasm); license compatibility (Apache-2 flow upstream). To be discussed, not built, pre-1.0.
